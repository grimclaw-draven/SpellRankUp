# Changelog

## 1.1.0 – animation

- Flight animation like learning a new spell: the icon appears at the trainer (or in the middle of the screen), flies to the action button and makes it light up
- `/spellrankup anim` turns the animation on or off

## 1.0.0 – first release

- Newly learned spell ranks replace the old rank on your action bars automatically
- Waits until combat is over; `/spellrankup on|off`
